STAR ARMY vs. THE BUGS

Bugs have taken over the computer! Build an army, protect your Star Tower, and seal up The Crack in the wall where they come from.

  • Hire Miners ⛏ to dig stars ★ out of the ore rocks. Gold ore far away gives the most, but it's dangerous! Squashing bugs gives a few stars too.
  • Tap a card at the bottom to send that fighter into battle.
  • Build Wall Cannons on your tower. They turn and shoot by themselves, and you can tap one for a super shot!
  • Lightning zaps every bug on the screen at once!
  • Use ATTACK and DEFEND at the top to tell your army to charge or guard the tower.
  • You start with only Swordy. Win stages to unlock new soldiers and earn ◆ gems for upgrades!
  • 30 stages, with a BOSS every 5 stages!
  • Tap one of your soldiers to control it yourself! Use ◀ ▶ to move and HIT to attack.
  • More modes: VS a friend or the computer, Zombie Attack, and Survive Till Sunrise!

Keyboard: 1–7 cards · Space fires a cannon · A attack · D defend · H control a soldier, ← → move, ↑ hit, ↓ let go

Tip: turn your phone sideways for a bigger battlefield
