Privacy Policy

Applies to all games on this site and in the apps made from them. Last updated: November 9, 2026 (Hide & Seek Online; Paper Adventure live player count).

The short version

Our games do not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, and no third-party analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

Game progress (such as unlocked stages, gems, upgrades, and trophies) and settings (such as sound on/off) are saved only on your own device, using your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. You can erase it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser, or by uninstalling the app.

Offline files

To let the games work without an internet connection, your browser keeps a copy of the game files (pictures, code, sounds). These are the same files everyone downloads and contain nothing about you.

Hosting

The website is hosted on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes basic technical information (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse. We do not use this information to identify or track players. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Online play (Star Army Online and Hide & Seek Online)

Star Army Online lets two players on two devices play together. One player makes a room and gets a random 4-letter code; the other player types the code to join. Each player picks a fun nickname from a fixed list (such as "Captain Comet"); you cannot type your own, so no real names are shared. While you play, the two devices send each other game moves (such as which soldier was sent and where the soldiers are) and the picked nickname through a small relay that runs on our own Cloudflare account. There are no real names, accounts, profiles, chat, or pictures. The last nickname you picked is remembered only on your own device. Star Army, Star Army Online, Paper Adventure, Hide & Seek, Hide & Seek Online and the games page show how many players are playing right now (Hide & Seek only reads that number; it sends nothing). To count single-player Star Army and Paper Adventure, each game sends an anonymous "still playing" signal about once a minute while it is open on the screen: it holds only a random number that the game makes up fresh every time it is opened (it is not saved on your device), and it is deleted from our server a couple of minutes after the game is closed. It has no names, no cookies and no device details, and it is used for nothing except that live number. Offline play is not affected; that is a single number counted from the open rooms, and it is not linked to anyone. The relay does not save anything: messages are passed straight to the other player, and the room disappears when the game ends. As with the website itself, Cloudflare processes basic technical information (such as IP address) to make the connection. Only someone who knows your room code can join, and a room holds just two players. Hide & Seek Online works the same way, with the same rooms, codes and fixed nickname list: the two devices send each other where each player is standing, which way they are facing, their pose (standing, crouching, lying down), the paint colors they picked and where they painted, and the picked nickname. All other games work without any network connection.

Children

These games are made for kids and families. Because we collect no personal information, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 or anyone else.

Links

The games contain no links to other websites except links back to this site.

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
